No Surprises

Honest, Upfront Pricing

Nobody likes surprises on the final bill. So Sherman HVAC prices every Sherman job before the work begins, after a complete diagnostic that covers the equipment's age, fuel type, and tonnage, refrigerant levels, coil and heat exchanger condition, and the ductwork feeding each room. You'll know the problem, what the fix involves, and what it costs before you say yes.

Many HVAC problems stay hidden until a failure is well underway. Refrigerant seeps out through pinhole leaks in the evaporator coil, blower bearings wear quietly, and a weakening capacitor gives no warning until the compressor won't start on the first hot day. If you notice weak airflow, rising energy bills, ice on the refrigerant lines, or rooms that never reach the thermostat setting, call for an inspection sooner rather than later. An early diagnosis often keeps a repairable issue from becoming a replacement.

How old is your system? When a furnace or AC gets past the 12-year mark, it's worth planning the next one before the current one decides for you. Emergency replacements happen on the worst days, in heat waves and cold snaps, when choices are limited and everyone is uncomfortable. Our team knows how the Sherman climate wears on equipment and will tell you plainly whether yours has years left or is running on borrowed time.

Preventive Care

Maintenance Plans for Sherman Homes

The best repair is the one you never need. That's the whole point of our maintenance plans. On each scheduled visit, we clean coils and burners, confirm the refrigerant charge, test the electrical components, inspect the heat exchanger, flush the condensate drain, and swap in a new filter, all matched to the equipment in your home.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with spring cooling tune-ups and fall heating tune-ups for most systems. That's especially important in dusty areas, where dirty condenser coils choke heat rejection and drive up energy use. Each home is assessed individually, and our technicians tailor the plan to your equipment's age, your household's comfort demands, and your local climate.

The savings show up faster than most people expect. Clean coils move heat with less effort, a correct refrigerant charge keeps the compressor from overworking, and a fresh filter spares the blower motor. Next to the cost of replacing a system even one year early, a maintenance plan is a bargain.

Right-Sized Systems

The Right Size System for Your Home

When it comes to HVAC, bigger is not better. An oversized unit short-cycles, burns extra energy, and never runs long enough to pull humidity out of the air, while an undersized one runs constantly and still can't keep up on the hottest days. That's why we never just copy the tonnage of the old unit. We run a proper load calculation that factors in your square footage, insulation, windows, and ductwork, and for mini-splits we size each head to the room it serves.

We walk you through the options for every home, especially growing families with rising comfort demands. A single-stage system that served a small household rarely satisfies a finished basement and two added bedrooms, and a modern two-stage or variable-speed heat pump can hold tighter temperatures while cutting energy use significantly. If you're weighing furnace-and-AC against a heat pump, or ducted against ductless, our technician will lay out the honest trade-offs: SEER2 efficiency ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting requirements, and upfront installation work, so you can pick what fits your home and budget.

Frequent Failures

The HVAC Repairs Sherman Homes Need Most

No cooling, a furnace that refuses to light, ice on the indoor coil, a slow refrigerant leak: every one of these problems has its own cause and needs its own repair. Sherman HVAC tracks down the exact fault before swapping any parts, whether it's a condenser that hums but won't start, a short-cycling furnace, a thermostat that keeps losing its program, a clogged condensate drain, or a mini-split flashing error codes.

Some problems are mostly a nuisance, while others pose real risks. A weak capacitor simply keeps the compressor from starting, but a cracked heat exchanger can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling. We match the fix to the failure: capacitors, contactors, and fan motors on cooling systems, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards on furnaces, sealed and repaired ductwork where conditioned air is bleeding into the attic, and a proper refrigerant recharge after a repaired leak. Whatever you're facing, our team has the parts and experience to handle it.
